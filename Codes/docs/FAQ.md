# Miss You — FAQ (draft, user-facing)

**Is this really them?** No. It's an AI recreation made from the photos, videos, voice notes and memories you share. It can feel close, but it is not them, and it will always tell you so if you ask.

**Will it pretend they're alive, or speaking from heaven?** Never. By default it doesn't use afterlife language at all. You can allow gentle, respectful spiritual words, but it will still never claim real contact.

**What happens to my photos and recordings?** They're encrypted on our server and shown only through short-lived private links. They're used to make your recreation and nothing else. You can download everything, or delete everything, at any time.

**Why does it say "AI" everywhere?** Because honesty matters, and Indian law requires synthetic media to be clearly labelled.

**What if a call makes me feel worse?** Please stop whenever you like. If you're in distress the call pauses and shows people you can talk to right now (Tele-MANAS 14416). There's a daily time limit, and nothing here is designed to keep you hooked.

**Why can't I recreate a child or a celebrity?** To protect children and to prevent impersonation, Miss You only recreates adults you knew personally.

**Does it work in Bengali and Hindi?** Yes — calls work in English, Bengali and Hindi, including mixed speech. Bengali voice cloning uses Sarvam AI.

**Why is lip movement "approximate"?** On this server their mouth moves with a talking animation timed to the voice rather than frame-accurate lip-sync, which needs a GPU or a specialist provider.

**What do credits pay for?** Making a scene (once per place, then reused), call minutes, voice cloning and letters. You always see the cost before anything is charged.
